About

I'm Yifan (亦凡) Yuan (袁), and I work on hardware–software co-design and technology pathfinding for hyperscale datacenter and AI infrastructure at Meta's AI Systems Co-design team, taking new technologies from early pathfinding and silicon definition through to fleet-wide production. My work spans CPUs, DPUs, memory, storage, and networking, and I co-developed DCPerf, Meta's open-source benchmark suite for datacenter workloads.

Before Meta, I spent two years at Intel Labs (Systems Architecture Lab) as a research scientist, where I led the first studies of CXL memory and CXL Type-2 devices on real hardware. I received my PhD from ECE@UIUC (advised by Prof. Nam Sung Kim), and my undergraduate degree from Zhejiang University (ZJU), where I focused on computer architecture and VLSI design.

I grew up in Tianjin, a city in China with a spirit of humor and optimism. I enjoy badminton, hiking, and cooking.

For my latest resume, please email me at yifany3 AT gmail DOT com.
